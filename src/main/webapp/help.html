Staff User Guide

Step-by-step instructions for managing patients, appointments and billing.

🔐 1. Login to System

1
Enter your authorized username and password.
2
Click Login.
3
After successful authentication, the Staff Dashboard will open.

👤 2. Register Patient Details

1
Open the Register Appointment page.
2
Enter the patient's full name, address and contact number.
3
Verify the entered details before continuing.

📅 3. Create New Appointment

1
Open Register Appointment from the Staff Dashboard.
2
Select the required dentist and treatment.
3
Select an appointment date and time.
4
Click Book Appointment.
5
The appointment information will be stored in the system.

🔍 4. Search Appointments

1
Open Search Appointment from the Staff Dashboard.
2
Search using the available appointment information.
3
Review the patient, dentist, treatment, date, time and status.
4
Select the required appointment before generating a bill.

💰 5. Create Patient Bill

1
Open the billing page for the required appointment.
2
Verify the appointment number.
3
Enter the treatment cost.
4
The system automatically calculates:  Total Amount = Consultation Fee + Treatment Cost
5
Click Create Bill to save the billing information.

🧾 6. Patient Receipt

1
Open the generated patient bill.
2
Verify the bill information and total amount.
3
Print or save the receipt if the system provides the print option.

🚪 7. Logout

1
Finish all required tasks.
2
Click Logout from the Staff Dashboard.
3
Do not share your username or password with unauthorized users.

⚠️ Important Rules

  • Verify patient information before creating an appointment.
  • Do not create duplicate bills for the same appointment.
  • Check dentist and treatment details carefully.
  • Keep patient information confidential.
  • Only authorized staff members should access the system.
  • Always logout after completing your work.
